# Prior-art differentiation table (v4 plan, item 1.2)

One row per paper adjacent to N1 (risk-controlled threshold triage). Carries the methods-venue submission later — this is the table a reviewer who knows the field will ask for, so it is written for that reviewer, not for padding.

| Paper | What it does | What N1 does that it does not |
|---|---|---|
| **Lambert et al., *Robust Conformal Volume Estimation in 3D Medical Images*, MICCAI 2024** (arXiv:2407.19938) | Conformal predictive intervals on volumes derived from 3D segmentation; a multi-head architecture emitting restrictive/permissive/balanced masks as the lower/upper bound plus a point estimate. This is the closest prior work to this project's *step 1* (the conformal predictive system) and is used here as **baseline 4** (§7) — marginal conformal interval, no threshold conditioning — the baseline N1 must beat. | Lambert et al. stop at the interval. There is no decision rule built on top of it, no certified risk, and no clinical asymmetry — their evaluation is interval width and marginal coverage on the volume itself, not error at a guideline cutoff. N1 turns their interval into a certified, asymmetric-risk triage decision (§4, step 2) and evaluates the decision's own error, not the interval's width. |
| **Jaubert et al., ISBI 2025** (arXiv:2506.04030) | Cluster-conditional conformal intervals on coronary calcium volume via histogram clustering, motivated by coverage failing under cohort/image-quality variation — the closest prior work to v3's old N1b (dropped, §0 of the v4 proposal). | Cluster-conditional coverage is still a coverage guarantee, not a decision guarantee — the same gap this project's §3 identifies. N1 does not condition coverage on anything; it certifies the triage decision's two asymmetric risks directly, and the conditioning that matters here (small V<τ vs. V≥τ groups) is handled through LTT's per-group sample-size accounting, not through clustering. |
| **Conformal selective prediction with cost-aware deferral for safe clinical triage under distribution shift**, *Scientific Reports* 2026 (DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-40637-w) | Establishes the general pipeline: conformal set → deferral threshold → risk-coverage curve, in a clinical triage setting, with shift-awareness. Nearest prior art to this project's overall shape and must be differentiated explicitly in any submission. | This is a **classification** deferral pipeline. N1's decision variable is a continuous, segmentation-derived quantity (infarct volume) compared to a guideline threshold, not a class label — the "score" being thresholded is itself the output of an upstream regression/segmentation model with its own uncertainty, which is what makes the CPS step (§4, step 1) necessary before any risk control can happen. N1 also certifies **two** risks simultaneously via a structural decoupling (§4, step 2) rather than one deferral risk. |
| **Boström & Johansson, *Mondrian conformal regressors*, COPA 2020** | The general Mondrian conformal construction: partition calibration data by a function of X, compute a separate conformity quantile per partition, retain per-partition coverage validity. This is exactly what v3's old N1b (distance-to-threshold binning) reduces to — cited explicitly to concede the point, not to claim past it. | N1 does not use Mondrian partitioning as its primary method. The retained diagnostic (§4, N1, "retained") still measures near-boundary coverage, but the *correction* is Learn-then-Test risk control on the decision, not a Mondrian-conditioned interval. Boström & Johansson's machinery is textbook and is cited as such; N1's contribution is the risk-control layer built on top of the CPD, which is a different object from a conditioned interval. |
| **Angelopoulos, Bates et al., *Learn then Test: Calibrating Predictive Algorithms to Achieve Risk Control*, Ann. Appl. Stat. 19(2), 2025** (arXiv:2110.01052) | The general LTT framework: treat "is parameter λ safe?" as a family of hypothesis tests, certify with FWER control across a λ grid via Hoeffding-Bentkus p-values. The statistical machinery `src/conformal/ltt.py` implements directly. | LTT itself is not claimed as novel — it is textbook machinery (§4, "not claimed as novel"). N1's contribution is (a) applying it to a **guideline threshold on a segmentation-derived continuous quantity** rather than a generic bounded loss, (b) the structural proof that this specific decision rule's two risks decouple by construction (R_missed depends only on λ_hi, R_futile only on λ_lo), which collapses the general multi-dimensional grid-search-plus-correction into two independent 1-D fixed-sequence walks — a design choice this project's own Bonferroni ablation shows is not optional at n=149 (dense-grid Bonferroni certifies nothing at this sample size), and (c) making the abstention band itself the certified clinical object, which the base paper's hand-set τ=0.15 is not. |
| **Gibbs, Cherian, Candès, *Conformal prediction with conditional guarantees*, JRSS-B 87(4):1100, 2025** | Principled conditional conformal coverage — a stronger, more general conditional guarantee than Mondrian binning, held in reserve as the fallback if this project's boundary diagnostic shows severe heterogeneity the certified policy handles poorly. | Not currently in the critical path (v4 plan §5, "implementation notes"). Cited to show the stronger conditional-coverage alternative was considered and explicitly not chosen as the primary method, because — per §3 of the proposal — conditional *coverage*, however principled, is still not a guarantee on the *decision*, which is what N1 targets directly. |
| **Cost-sensitive conformal prediction benchmark, arXiv:2607.27143** | Benchmarks cost-sensitive conformal methods across classification tasks with asymmetric misclassification costs. | Classification-only, and benchmarks existing cost-sensitive methods rather than proposing a decision-level guarantee for a continuous, guideline-threshold clinical decision. Establishes that asymmetric-cost conformal methods are an active, contested area — supporting evidence that N1's asymmetric two-risk construction is a live question, not a solved one, in a setting (continuous decision variable, guideline threshold, segmentation-derived score) this benchmark does not cover. |

## What is left after subtracting all of the above

Per the v4 proposal §4: risk control applied to a **guideline threshold on a segmentation-derived continuous quantity with two-sided, guideline-set asymmetric clinical costs**, where the two risks are shown to decouple structurally (making the general multi-dimensional LTT machinery unnecessary) and the certified abstention band is itself the clinical deliverable. No single row in this table does all three of those things; several do one or two.
