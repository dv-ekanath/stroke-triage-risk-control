Methodology & limitations
Segmentation Dice is statistically indistinguishable between the baseline and graph-guided models (paired test, p = 0.59, n = 148 patients) — the graph is not presented as making segmentation more accurate. Its demonstrated value is twofold: across the full training grid the graph-guided model never collapsed to a near-empty prediction (0/9 runs) where the plain baseline did twice (2/9) — try "Failed training run" above to see that failure mode directly — and the trust check shown on each panel, built from admission occlusion side and predicted volume with no ground truth required, detects severe segmentation failure with large effect (AUROC 0.84) and a wide margin over a standard ensemble-disagreement baseline (AUROC 0.58). Patients here are the 50 held-out ISLES'24 validation subjects; none of these models trained on them. Uploading identifies which held-out patient you mean from the filename and runs inference on our preprocessed copy of their data — it does not process a genuinely new, unprocessed scan (that would additionally require a perfusion pipeline and a vessel-segmentation model this project does not include). Full numbers in outputs/tables/ and paper/kgxai_paper.tex.
